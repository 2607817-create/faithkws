Kenya Wildlife Service (KWS)

About KWS

The Kenya Wildlife Service (KWS) is a government organization responsible for protecting and conserving Kenya's wildlife and their habitats. KWS manages national parks, reserves, and other protected areas across the country.

Wildlife and Conservation

Kenya is home to many amazing animals, including elephants, lions, giraffes, rhinos, and buffaloes. KWS works to protect these animals and preserve Kenya's natural environment for future generations.
